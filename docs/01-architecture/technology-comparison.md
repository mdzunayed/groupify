# Technology comparison and remaining decisions

This comparison brings the Phase 1 decisions together for review. It records the owner's confirmed choices separately from candidates; it does not claim that a provider has been purchased, deployed, or validated on a device. The [cost and capacity research](research-cost-capacity.md) contains the pricing sources, workload variables, and limits of the available estimates.

| Area | Options and trade-offs | Status and evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Android client | Expo/React Native preserves the existing app; native Kotlin modules and development builds are still needed where platform behavior requires them. | **Approved:** Expo/React Native is primary. Room, Coroutines/Flow, Material 3 and the native boundary need compatibility and device evidence. [ADR-001](architecture-decisions/ADR-001-client-stack.md), [ADR-013](architecture-decisions/ADR-013-native-integration.md). |
| Backend | NestJS shares TypeScript with the client and supports HTTP/WebSockets; Ktor, Spring and Go were alternatives. A modular monolith reduces deployment complexity but couples releases and failure domains. | **Approved:** TypeScript/NestJS modular monolith with PostgreSQL/PostGIS, Redis and WebSockets. Schema, deployment and measured capacity remain open. [ADR-002](architecture-decisions/ADR-002-backend.md), [ADR-008](architecture-decisions/ADR-008-capacity.md). |
| Hosting | A self-managed VM needs patching, backups and restore ownership; managed services shift work to recurring provider costs. Neither a compute-only estimate nor one region proves the full budget. | **Open:** provider, region, topology, staging, backups and availability objectives. Compare complete quotes against the ৳2,000–৳3,000 monthly planning allowance. [ADR-003](architecture-decisions/ADR-003-hosting.md). |
| Phone identity | Clerk already supports the current development flow; Firebase, Twilio Verify and a suitable local gateway are comparison candidates with different delivery and operational responsibilities. | **Approved for development:** Clerk. **Open for production:** provider and SMS route, Bangladesh carrier coverage, sender registration, rates, recovery and abuse controls. [ADR-004](architecture-decisions/ADR-004-otp-identity.md). |
| Maps and routing | MapLibre plus licensed tiles and a separate routing/geocoding service can separate providers; integrated or self-hosted options change operating work and cost. [ADR-005](architecture-decisions/ADR-005-mapping.md). |
| PTT media | A managed WebRTC service reduces some operations; self-hosted WebRTC adds SFU/TURN, bandwidth and support work. Prove single-speaker media enforcement and background/locked-screen audio on physical devices. | GPS and offline sync | Adaptive sampling can balance freshness against battery, bandwidth and storage; local recording and later synchronization need explicit state and conflict rules. | **Approved:** active-ride opt-in, background/screen-off tracking subject to Android constraints, and distinct local/uploaded/remote states. **Open:** thresholds, synchronization rules, retention trigger and device evidence. [ADR-009](architecture-decisions/ADR-009-gps-sampling.md), [ADR-011](architecture-decisions/ADR-011-offline-sync.md). |
| Billing | Google Play Billing with server verification is a candidate; provider and policy choices affect fees, restoration and operations. | **Open:** payment provider, prices, limits and premium matrix. SOS, essential communication and basic navigation remain free. [ADR-010](architecture-decisions/ADR-010-subscriptions.md). |

## Device proof status

On 2026-11-11, the cloud environment had Java but no `adb` or Android emulator command available. No physical-device connection or development build was available to test. Neither the [PTT nor background-location protocol](device-validation-plan.md) has been run, so their proof-of-concept reports and the Phase 1 device evidence remain **blocked**, not passed. The Android JavaScript export verifies bundling only.

Next obtain a supported Android device/build and the approved media candidate, then run and record each protocol separately. Do not infer background behavior, microphone reliability, or received audio from a simulator, a mock, or a successful bundle.
