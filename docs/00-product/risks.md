# Product and technical risks

Each item below is a **Risk**, not an approved change or a claim that the failure has occurred. The linked [open decisions](open-decisions.md) remain unresolved until explicitly decided; mitigations are evaluation topics rather than new implementation requirements.

| ID | Risk | What needs evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | A disconnected or interrupted PTT speaker could leave the channel locked and prevent others from speaking. | Stale-session detection and recovery, alongside priority behavior and the rule against an automatic speaking-duration timeout (OD-13). |
| R-02 | Android background microphone and locked-screen restrictions could prevent the required PTT experience. | Device and platform validation, including background and locked-screen operation (OD-08). |
| R-03 | Mapping licensing, provider limits, or costs could make the intended map experience unavailable or unaffordable. | Map rendering, tile, routing, and geocoding providers, license terms, and expected usage (OD-05). |
| R-04 | OTP, hosting, maps, messaging, or audio costs could exceed the ৳2,000–৳3,000 monthly infrastructure planning allowance. | Realistic usage and provider estimates; the allowance is not a confirmed price or a hard usage limit (OD-03–OD-06, OD-09). |
| R-05 | Unclear community-versus-ride permission boundaries could give the wrong people leadership, communication, emergency, or location access. | Membership, role assignment, leadership contexts, and the missing permissions in the [matrix](business-rules.md#role-and-permission-matrix) (OD-14, OD-15). |
| R-06 | Offline synchronization conflicts, delayed uploads, or confusing local versus server status could produce missing, stale, or inconsistent location information. | Conflict policy, delayed synchronization, last-known display, and evidence of server receipt (OD-12, OD-16). |
| R-07 | Sensitive location, SOS, authentication, or account-deletion data could be exposed or retained longer than permitted, including in local copies or shared records. | Access controls and enforcement of the approved location and deletion periods; legal retention boundaries require determination (OD-15–OD-18). |
| R-08 | Expo/React Native must integrate the retained Room, Coroutines, Flow, and Material 3 directions, and the existing Clerk integration may be mistaken for an approved final OTP/SMS provider. | Validate native-module integration and obtain approved ADRs before dependent implementation; the client-stack conflict is resolved, but the OTP provider is still open (OD-01, OD-04). |
| R-09 | The app design reference could lead to press-and-release PTT or ambiguous nearby-rider, Admin, or SOS behavior inconsistent with or beyond the approved baseline. | Align the references with approved behavior and resolve the open details (OD-18, OD-19). |
| R-10 | An SOS with unavailable or stale connectivity or location could be misunderstood as successfully delivered with current location. | Define edge cases and distinguish available evidence of local action, actual alert delivery, and live versus last-known location (OD-18). |
| R-11 | Background/screen-off GPS may be unreliable on supported Android devices, and an incorrect legal-retention interpretation could retain location data too long or delete required records. | Validate the confirmed background requirement and determine applicable legal obligations without treating the exception as a blanket extension (OD-16, OD-17, OD-25; [ADR-001](../01-architecture/architecture-decisions/ADR-001-client-stack.md)). |
| R-12 | An OTP option may lack acceptable Bangladesh carrier delivery, sender registration, abuse protection, or sustainable per-message costs. | Obtain provider-specific quotes and test representative carrier routes before approval (OD-04; [ADR-004](../01-architecture/architecture-decisions/ADR-004-otp-identity.md)). |
| R-13 | A control-plane stale-lock decision may leave an old media publisher active, allowing overlapping speakers during partitions or reconnects. | Prove media-enforced fencing and liveness behavior in device and concurrent failure tests; avoid a speaking-duration timeout (OD-13; [ADR-012](../01-architecture/architecture-decisions/ADR-012-ptt-arbitration.md)). |
| R-14 | A push provider accepting a message may be mistaken for a device receiving an emergency alert. | Treat local action, durable server acceptance, notification handoff, device receipt and user read as distinct evidence (OD-18; [ADR-014](../01-architecture/architecture-decisions/ADR-014-realtime-notifications.md)). |
| R-15 | A native Room/Kotlin candidate may conflict with the eventual supported Android range or Expo toolchain, and native execution can fail when the JavaScript UI is unavailable. | Check compatible SDK/Kotlin/KSP/Room versions and test real devices before approving the native boundary (OD-07, OD-25; [ADR-013](../01-architecture/architecture-decisions/ADR-013-native-integration.md)). |
| R-16 | The existing dependency graph reports moderate security advisories and deprecated packages; npm's automatic suggestion would substantially downgrade the Expo/Clerk stack. | Review actual exposure and SDK-compatible fixes during version selection; do not apply automatic major downgrades ([development foundation](../05-development/foundation.md#repository-and-commands), OD-07). |
